Further research highlights advancements in &quot;TuringSpace: Grounding Spatial Intelligence in Action with Interactive CAPTCHAs&quot; and &quot;Mind2Act World&quot; for robotic manipulation, signaling a broader push towards embodied AI and sophisticated interaction models.</p>
