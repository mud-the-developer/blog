    <p class="news-digest-section-description" data-pretext-target>GitHub activity remains robust, with NousResearch/hermes-agent leading the velocity charts. This project, described as &quot;the agent that grows with you,&quot; continues to attract substantial attention, accumulating over 217,000 stars and adding 800 in the last week alone. Other agent-focused repositories, including BerriAI/litellm and openai/codex, also show strong momentum, indicating a sustained developer interest in agentic frameworks, AI gateways, and coding assistants. The emergence of projects like code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent and anomalyco/opencode further underscores the active development in open-source coding agents.</p>
    <p class="news-digest-section-description" data-pretext-target>In research, the paper &quot;RESOURCE2SKILL: Distilling Executable Agent Skills from Human-Created Multimodal Resources&quot; has garnered considerable attention. This work explores the crucial abstraction of skills for software agents, aiming to transform human and agent experience into reusable procedural knowledge. This focus on practical skill distillation aligns with the broader trend of making AI agents more capable and adaptable. Other notable papers include &quot;xHC: Expanded Hyper-Connections,&quot; which proposes memory scaling beyond traditional model width and depth, and &quot;Cura 1T,&quot; a specialized model for agentic healthcare, reflecting diverse applications of advanced AI.</p>
    <p class="news-digest-section-description" data-pretext-target>Community discussions are actively tracking OpenAI&#x27;s recent decision to reduce the Codex model&#x27;s context size from 372k to 272k. This adjustment has sparked conversations on platforms like GeekNews, with developers and enthusiasts weighing the implications for coding tasks and model performance. Related discussions also highlight practical challenges, such as Codex CLI&#x27;s subagent session logs consuming significant disk space, and the need for tools like &quot;sessionhub&quot; to manage scattered coding sessions. These conversations underscore the real-world operational considerations and developer tooling needs surrounding large language models.</p>
    <p class="news-digest-section-description" data-pretext-target>Beyond specific projects and papers, a recurring theme across the ecosystem is the pursuit of efficiency and practical application. Whether it&#x27;s through distilling agent skills, optimizing model architectures with hyper-connections, or managing the resource demands of coding agents, the community is actively seeking ways to enhance AI utility and address operational challenges. The attention on context window changes for models like Codex also points to an ongoing refinement of model capabilities and their practical deployment.</p>
