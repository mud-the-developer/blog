# Obsidian Linking Habits

Connected writing stays maintainable when links are explicit.

## Link Types

- `[[note]]` for direct references
- `[[note|label]]` for readable anchor text
- `[[note#heading]]` for deep links into long notes

Examples:

- Start from [[home]]
- Deployment process: [[github-cloudflare-pipeline]]
- Internal architecture: [[second-brain#Second Brain Architecture|architecture intro]]
