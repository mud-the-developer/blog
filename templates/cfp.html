Each weekly issue is generated by mud-blog cfp update, then validated by mud-blog cfp validate before commit. Journal rows use annually refreshed JCR/SJR-style quartile metadata and impact-factor fields; conference rows do not use journal Impact Factors and instead record CORE/society/field-ranking proxies.
